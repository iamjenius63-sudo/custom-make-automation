🛠️ Step-by-Step Construction Guide

Step 1: Funnel Architecture Setup

Click Funnels → Create. Enter a title, select your currency, and choose the Sell objective. Systeme.io automatically sets up an Order Form and a Thank You page.

Step 2: Designing Your Order Form

Select a conversion-optimized checkout template. Add your product mockup image, customer testimonials, secure payment badges, and a 30-day money-back guarantee seal.

Step 3: Configuring the 1-Click Order Bump

Under the Order Form elements, drag an Order Bump block. Set an irresistible low-ticket impulse add-on (e.g., an audio version or checklist for $17). Over 30% of buyers check this box, dramatically increasing Average Order Value (AOV).

Step 4: Adding a Post-Purchase Upsell

Click Add Step → Upsell. Create a 1-click offer presented immediately after checkout. If accepted, the customer's card is charged instantly without re-entering payment details.
